About

I am a researcher at the Universitat de les Illes Balears (UIB) Meteorology Group and teach in the Physics Department, where I hold an Assistant Professor position.

I work on mesoscale meteorology and high-impact weather in the Western Mediterranean, from meteotsunami prediction and severe convective environments to snowfall events over complex terrain. My work combines high-resolution numerical modelling (MM5, WRF, TRAM, CM1), neural network approaches applied to real meteorological problems, and statistical downscaling methods for climate change impact assessment.

For several years I led R&D at Meteoclim Services, a UIB spin-off supported by a Torres Quevedo fellowship, applying climate science to tourism, energy and water management. The perspective I gained during this time still underlies both my research and my teaching.
